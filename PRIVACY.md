# QuestBridge Privacy Policy

Mac App Store edition — updated October 17, 2026

Contact: mingistech@mac.com

QuestBridge transfers files between your Mac and your connected headset. It has
no online account, advertising, analytics or tracking service. It does not send
your transferred files, filenames or headset identifiers to developer servers.

## Local Files And Settings

QuestBridge reads files and folders you select, lists shared headset storage,
and writes the transfers you request. File sizes, timestamps and available disk
space support browsing and safe transfers. Original upload sources remain
untouched. Rename, replacement and deletion apply to the items you choose.

Preferences, device selection, remembered folder-access bookmarks and ADB
authorization keys are stored locally. This edition keeps its keys inside its
macOS app container. Do not send the private authorization key to support.

Transfers use temporary local staging copies. Normal operation cleans them up;
an abrupt app or system exit can leave a copy until it is removed or macOS
purges temporary storage. Completed transfers stay on your devices until you
remove them.

## Diagnostics And Notifications

Diagnostic details may include paths, filenames or device information. They
appear locally in Settings and private-marked macOS error logs. QuestBridge does
not automatically upload them. Review and redact anything you choose to share.
macOS crash-reporting and backup behavior follow your system settings and
Apple's policies.

Optional queue-completion notifications are local. Disable them in QuestBridge
Settings or macOS notification settings. QuestBridge uses no remote notification
service.

## Connections And Choices

The app communicates with its bundled connection helper over private loopback
on your Mac and with your headset over USB. The Store edition has no GitHub
update checker; updates are handled through Apple's services.

You choose transfer sources and destinations through file selection or drag and
drop. Choose another default download folder in Settings or revoke USB debugging
authorization on the headset. Local settings, bookmarks and keys live in this
edition's app container. Removing that data resets the corresponding settings
and authorization; quit first and check for any wanted temporary copies.

## Support And Other Services

For support, email mingistech@mac.com. Sending a report is your choice. Include
only the information needed to explain the issue and avoid sending private
media, identifiers or authorization keys. Mail services and any third-party
websites you choose to use have their own policies. Meta's account and headset
services are governed by Meta's policies.

This policy applies to the Store edition. The separately distributed GitHub
edition has different update-check and external-helper behavior.

## GitHub edition

The GitHub edition also offers manual and periodic update checks against GitHub
Releases. GitHub receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP
address, under its own privacy policy. Transferred files are not sent in update
checks. This edition permits an external ADB executable, whose behavior depends
on the executable you select; authorization-key storage can differ from the
App Store edition.
